FAQ

Questions & Answers

How does payment work?

Bidding takes a 20% refundable deposit, paid by card when you place the bid. If you don't win, it comes back in full; if you do, it counts toward the price and I send a link for the remainder.

What if someone outbids me?

You get an email alert the moment you're outbid plus the chance to swing back. Outbids must beat the current top bid by at least A$10.

What do I actually get?

A die-cut vinyl logo on the lid — visible in person and in the travel/work photos I post for the life of the machine (5–6 years) — plus a linked spot on this page. No impressions are promised, but it's real, founder-led placement that keeps showing up.

What logo file do you need?

Upload it when you bid — no logo, no bid. SVG vector is strongly preferred, or a transparent PNG at print quality. You approve a placement mock before anything goes on the lid, and you keep all rights to your logo.

Can any brand join?

Almost. Every sponsor is approved by hand and I keep the final say on what travels with me. Refused bids get their deposit back in full.

What about the floor and the tax?

Below A$6,000 everything is refunded in full — no Mac, no charge. Above it, ~32.5% goes to Australian income tax before I buy anything; if the raise still falls short of the machine, I cover the difference. Full P&L is published at the end.

Terms

Participation terms.

These are the plain-English terms that govern bidding on Brand the Device.

  • The auction. 14 ad spots on the lid of one 16-inch MacBook Pro M5 Max (128GB / 2TB, nano-texture). The highest accepted bid per spot at the close wins. The close time is shown on the page countdown.
  • Funding, not ownership. The founder does not currently own the laptop. All proceeds go toward buying it; if the raise falls short, the founder tops up the difference so the exact listed machine ships.
  • Floor & refunds. A$6,000 is the go/no-go floor. If the auction settles below it, every deposit is refunded in full and no machine is purchased. Above the floor, deposits are non-refundable except as stated here.
  • Deposits. A winning bid requires a 20% refundable deposit at the time you bid. If you are outbid, your deposit is returned in full. If you win, it counts toward your total and you're invoiced for the remainder.
  • Manual approval. The founder reviews every sponsor and may refuse a bid for any reason; refused bids are refunded in full. The founder has final say on what appears on the lid.
  • Your logo. You retain all rights to your logo. You grant the founder a limited licence to print it as a vinyl sticker on this one lid and show it in photos/posts of that lid. You approve a placement mock before printing.
  • No guarantee of results. The founder makes no promise of impressions, clicks, ROI, or audience size. You're buying placement on a real, travelling laptop, not advertising metrics.
  • Tax. Amounts are in AUD. The founder is responsible for Australian income tax on the raise; any GST obligations (if registered) are handled separately. Sponsors should seek their own tax advice.
  • Governing law. These terms are governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia.

Privacy

What we collect, and why.

  • What you give us. When you bid: sponsor/company name, email, website URL (optional), logo file, and bid amount.
  • How we use it. Email is used for outbid alerts and the payment link. Sponsor name, website and logo are shown publicly on this page and on the laptop lid if your bid wins and is approved.
  • What we don't do. We don't sell your data, and we don't run third-party ad trackers on the bid form.
  • Storage. Bids are stored server-side to run the auction; you can request deletion of your personal data by messaging the founder on X.
  • Cookies. The site stores only local preferences (theme and currency choice) in your browser. No tracking cookies.
